VOICE BOOTH

Keep the voices you’ll miss hearing.

A private voice and photo guestbook for weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, and the gatherings worth remembering.

Made for meaningful events

Turn an iPhone or iPad into a shared voice booth. Every message can include one optional photo, and memories stay on the devices chosen by the host.

Private by design

  • No account
  • No cloud media upload
  • No advertising or tracking
  • No subscription

Guests with the app installed can send a voice message and one optional photo directly to a nearby host device. The host can replay, organize, and export event memories.
