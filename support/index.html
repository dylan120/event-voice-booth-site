Voice Booth Support

Voice Booth is a private, local-first event guestbook for voice messages and up to six optional photos with each message.

Contact support
dylan120liu@gmail.com
Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send private recordings or photos unless we explicitly ask you to.

Restore a purchase

Open the Host Lifetime purchase screen and tap Restore Purchases. You must use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase and be connected to the internet. Purchases are processed by Apple.

Keep your memories safe

On the host device, export an event through the app’s export option. The export includes original M4A audio, optional JPEG photos, CSV metadata, and a SHA-256 manifest. Keep a copy in a location you control.

Nearby guest messages

Nearby transfer requires both Apple devices to have Voice Booth installed, stay nearby, and remain in the foreground while sending. Check Local Network, Bluetooth, Microphone, and Camera permissions if a message cannot be sent.

Delete local data

Hosts can delete individual messages or events from within the app. Guests can delete an unsent draft from the Join Nearby screen. Deleting the app removes its local data unless it was exported or backed up elsewhere.

Privacy

Read the Voice Booth Privacy Policy for details about local storage, permissions, nearby transfer, and deletion.
