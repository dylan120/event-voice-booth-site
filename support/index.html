Voice Booth Support

Voice Booth is a private, local-first event guestbook for voice messages and up to six optional photos with each message.

Contact support
dylan120liu@gmail.com
Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send private recordings or photos unless we explicitly ask you to.

Restore a purchase

Open the Host Monthly purchase screen and tap Restore Purchases. You must use the same Apple Account that started the subscription or made the original Host Lifetime purchase and be connected to the internet. Manage or cancel a subscription in Apple Account subscription settings. Purchases are processed by Apple.

Keep your memories safe

On the host device, export an event through the app’s export option. The export includes original M4A audio, optional JPEG photos, CSV metadata, and a SHA-256 manifest. Keep a copy in a location you control.

Create a Memory Movie

Open an Event, find Keep & manage, and choose Export Memory Movie. Voice Booth creates a local 16:9, 1080p MOV from the Event’s voice messages and attached photos. Keep the app in the foreground while it is generating. If the export is cancelled or fails, check available storage and try again. The original messages are not changed.

Web Guest messages

When a Host enables a Web Guest QR session, guests can record in a supported browser and optionally add photos or a selfie effect. The session is short-lived. Once the Host stops the Event, the QR page is revoked and cannot accept new messages. The Host should use the app’s manual sync before stopping so received media can be imported and acknowledged before the 24-hour secure holding period ends.

Delete local data

Hosts can delete individual messages or events from within the app. Deleting the app removes its local data unless it was exported or backed up elsewhere.

Privacy

Read the Voice Booth Privacy Policy for details about local storage, Web Guest processing, permissions, and deletion.
