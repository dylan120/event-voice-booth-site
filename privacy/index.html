Voice Booth Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

Voice Booth is designed to keep event voice messages and optional photos on the devices used by the host and guests. We do not operate an account system or a cloud service for these messages.

Information handled by the app

When a user chooses to provide it, Voice Booth may handle voice recordings; up to six optional photos per voice message; an optional guest name and relationship; host-created event details; short-lived pairing information for nearby Apple devices; and purchase entitlement information supplied by Apple for the Host Lifetime in-app purchase.

How information is used

This information is used only for recording, nearby device-to-device transfer, local organization, playback, editing, recovery of an unsent guest draft, and host-controlled export. Selected or captured photos are re-encoded as JPEG files; Voice Booth removes source metadata such as GPS location, capture time, and device make/model from the stored copy.

Storage and transfer

  • Voice messages, optional photos, names, relationships, and event details are stored locally in the app’s protected storage.
  • Nearby submissions use Apple’s Multipeer Connectivity framework in an encrypted local session. Voice Booth does not upload media to a Voice Booth server.
  • Unsent nearby guest drafts remain on that guest device until the host confirms receipt or the guest deletes the draft.
  • Hosts choose if and where to export event files using the iOS share sheet. The destination provider’s terms and privacy practices apply after export.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data unless the user has exported or backed it up elsewhere.

Tracking and analytics

Voice Booth does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Permissions

  • Microphone: record a voice message.
  • Camera: scan an event QR code or take optional message photos.
  • Photos: choose up to six optional message photos through Apple’s system picker.
  • Local network and Bluetooth: discover and communicate with a nearby event device using transports selected by iOS.

Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. Disabling a permission may prevent its corresponding feature from working.

Purchases

Host Lifetime is a non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple. Voice Booth does not receive or store payment card information. Apple’s terms and privacy policy govern App Store transactions.

Children’s privacy

Voice Booth is not directed to children under 13. Event hosts are responsible for choosing participants and obtaining any consent required by applicable law.

User choices and deletion

Guests may delete an unsent draft from the Join Nearby screen. Hosts control messages stored on the host device and may delete or export them. Because Voice Booth has no account or server-side media store, we cannot remotely access, retrieve, or delete local event content.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected by updating this policy and its effective date before a new app version is released.
